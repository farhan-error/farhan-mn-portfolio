Defining Mobility Equity: A Dimension Based Framework for Assessing Urban Transport in Indian Cities

International Journal for Multidisciplinary Research (IJFMR) · Vol. 8, Issue 2

The study identifies and structures four core dimensions of mobility equity — accessibility, availability, affordability and safety — through a review of global and national transport frameworks and comparative assessment of six cities.
