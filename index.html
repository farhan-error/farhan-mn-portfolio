About

Engineering precision.
Planning vision.

I began in Civil Engineering — building an understanding of how infrastructure is designed, constructed, and made to last. Fieldwork on construction projects taught me to translate plans into reality and to appreciate the practical constraints that shape built environments. That foundation now informs everything I do as a planner: I don't just propose ideas, I understand what it takes to deliver them.

My planning work has focused on urban mobility equity, transport systems, smart infrastructure, and climate-resilient design. My thesis assessed mobility equity across Kochi's urban zones using GIS spatial analysis and primary surveys. My dissertation built an equity index framework benchmarked against six global cities. Alongside, I have designed smart city proposals for Ponnani and Ernakulam, and a distributed post-disaster recovery system for Alappuzha. My research on mobility equity has been published in a peer-reviewed journal. I am now seeking roles where analytical depth, cross-disciplinary thinking, and a genuine commitment to inclusive urbanism can make a difference.
